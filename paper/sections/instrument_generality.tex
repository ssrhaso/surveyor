\section{The Verification Gap: Applicability, Measured in Advance}
\label{sec:instrument}

The criterion floor of \Cref{sec:calib-tau} is half of a more general object:
the accept test must separate \emph{equivalence} distances (arrived, up to
task tolerance) from \emph{hop} distances (one subgoal interval apart, did not
move), both computable from cached latents alone. A workable threshold exists
iff $\mathcal{G} = [\,\mathrm{equiv}\ p_{90},\ \mathrm{hop}\ p_{10}\,]$ is
non-empty, and $\tau$ must lie inside it. We call $\mathcal{G}$ the
\textbf{verification gap} and compute it for every substrate this paper
touches (\Cref{fig:gap}, \Cref{app:generality}), one GPU-pass over cached
latents, minutes per environment.

\paragraph{One statistic explains every outcome.} PushT's $\tau{=}0.20$ sits
inside its gap $[0.171, 0.406]$: pure spec-accept wins with no auxiliary
mechanism. Cube's gap $[0.219, 0.353]$ sits just \emph{above} the transfer
$\tau$: slight over-rejection, and indeed Cube required the arrival gate.
Reacher's bulks separate $4\times$ but the tails overlap ($0.410$ vs.\
$0.379$): a noisy verifier, and indeed Reacher required the $c^{*}$ router.
TwoRoom-on-LeWM is \emph{inverted at every stride}: adjacent frames read
$0.88$ median while unrelated episodes read ${\approx}\sqrt{2}$; no threshold
exists (even ground-truth demo waypoints verify $14/113$). DROID's pooled space opens no gap at any stride, and the
ground-truth calibration row at that scale measures exactly what remains: a
statistic that rank-tracks and separates scenes while running metrically loose
at the transfer $\tau$ (\Cref{sec:results-cert}, \Cref{sec:generality-scale}).

\paragraph{Boundaries, tested against ourselves.} The applicability claim
(gap exists $\Rightarrow$ a $\tau$ exists; inverted $\Rightarrow$ verification
cannot operate) is distinct from a stronger midpoint-prescription we tested
and reject: pre-registered on Cube ($\tau{=}0.286$ at the midpoint, gate off),
SR \emph{fell} to $74.6$ against a $\ge78.0$ bar, so we claim applicability
and $\tau$-existence, not $\tau$-optimality. Where bulks separate but tails
overlap, a small time-contrastive \emph{lens} (verifier-side only) can reopen
the interval on held-out episodes (Reacher $-0.039 \to +0.031$; Maze
$+0.167$; Wall $+0.146$; TwoRoom-on-DINOv2 $+0.229$), with two honest
boundaries: DROID's pooled space resists the lens at any tested data scale
($8\times$ data, width $-0.107$; $20\times$ repairs neither drafter fidelity
nor planning value, both frozen predictions confirmed), and an open lens gap
is an \emph{offline} statement, the Reacher lens traded short-horizon SR for
verification efficiency and the pre-registered Wall closed-loop test could
not run (\Cref{sec:generality-dinowm}).

\section{Generality: A Second Architecture and a Scale Transplant}
\label{sec:generality}

The method transplants twice, the instrument predicting \emph{first} (full
narratives in \Cref{app:generality}).

\paragraph{DINO-WM (a second architecture).}\label{sec:generality-dinowm}
The instrument ran before any spec-accept cell on DINO-WM \citep{dinowm}:
PushT's gap is open but the transfer $\tau{=}0.20$ sits \emph{above} it, so
$\tau{=}0.121$ was derived and five predictions frozen before execution.
\textbf{P3 confirmed}, the cleanest prospective hit in the program: Wall at
the fixed transfer $\tau$ scores $0.685$ against flat's $1.00$, a $31$pp
underperformance called before the run. \textbf{P4 unavailable}: no lens gap
ever opened at Wall's serving hop, so the arm was not run. On PushT a
component-isolation autopsy exonerated the serving path and convicted the
transplanted \emph{drafter's content} at full data scale; the verifier's
${\sim}97\%$ rejection rate was the \emph{correct} reading of a bad drafter.
\textbf{P1, P2, and P5 are therefore unadjudicated-for-method}. Verifier,
derived $\tau$, and serving machinery transfer; generative drafting on frozen
video features has not, at any data scale we could buy.

\paragraph{TwoRoom (an encoder property, repairable).}\label{sec:generality-tworoom}
The instrument localizes TwoRoom's impossibility to the encoder, and the causal
test confirms: the \emph{same pixels} through frozen DINOv2 cure the saturation
($0.876 \to 0.081$; lens gap $+0.229$, derived $\tau{=}0.540$). Diagnosed in
advance and repaired: the LeWM-side closed loop recovers $+15.4$pp at range
(\Cref{sec:results-heldout}); the DINO-WM-side loop was cut at the
pre-declared deadline and remains a frozen prediction.

\paragraph{V-JEPA 2 ($55\times$ scale).}\label{sec:generality-scale}
The \emph{mechanism} carries: the certified source runs end-to-end, the
verifier constant transfers ($\tau{=}0.20$ against measured single-replan
floors of $0.06$--$0.10$), and the calibration audit runs unchanged at scale
($\rho=0.53$, cross-scene rejection $99.9\%$, dataset-pair label;
\Cref{sec:results-cert}). The scale boundary: $c^{*}$ carries no
discriminative signal (routes $0/36$ anchors to flat); the drafter is not
rescued by $20\times$ data; planning \emph{quality} cannot be honestly
adjudicated offline at all (\Cref{app:generality}). An exploratory
$15\times$-compute result did not survive the powered pre-registered
replication and is retired; closed-loop evaluation at scale is future
work.

\paragraph{Concurrent work.} SAGE \citep{sage} independently reports drafted
subgoal latents repairing long-horizon myopia on LeWM, converging evidence
for the premise, but consumes drafts open-loop; TRM \citep{trm} repairs
latent metrics at training time, the complement to our verifier-side lens.
Ours remains the certificate and the instrument.
